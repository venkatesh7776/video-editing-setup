# ONERENT — SIX VIDEO ADVERTISING SCRIPTS
## 30-day rental security deposit demand-validation campaign

**Brand:** ONERENT  
**Positioning:** The Financial Layer of Renting  
**Campaign objective:** Generate qualified early-access waitlist registrations and validate demand for rental security deposit financing in India.  
**Primary CTA:** Join the ONERENT early-access waitlist.  
**Creative style:** Premium fintech storytelling with clean motion graphics, relatable renter situations and Stripe-inspired visual clarity. Use original ONERENT assets; do not copy Stripe's branding.

## Campaign principles

The six videos test different motivations:

1. Master explainer — explain the problem and proposed solution.
2. Deposit problem — make the upfront deposit pain relatable.
3. Moving experience — connect with people relocating.
4. Financial flexibility — test the cash-flow planning angle.
5. Founder introduction — build human trust.
6. UGC-style renter scenario — test native social storytelling.

Produce the master explainer first. Reuse its characters, apartment scenes, product UI and visual assets in videos 2–4. Record the founder video separately. Shoot video 6 vertically in a natural social-media style.

**Important claims guardrails:** ONERENT is validating demand. Unless a regulated lending partner and operational product are in place, describe financing as an upcoming or proposed option being explored through regulated lending partners. Do not imply that ONERENT currently disburses loans. Do not promise guaranteed approval, instant disbursement, fixed rates or specific terms. Waitlist registration is not a loan application or approval. Do not invent testimonials, partnerships, customer numbers or results. Label conceptual UI as illustrative.

# VIDEO 01 — MASTER EXPLAINER

**Duration:** 30–35 seconds  
**Format:** Premium 2D/2.5D animation, character storytelling and conceptual product UI.  
**Priority:** Main campaign video.

**Core message:** Finding a home is exciting, but arranging a large security deposit upfront can be difficult. ONERENT is building a way for renters to explore rental security deposit financing options through regulated lending partners.

| Time | Visual direction | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–4 sec | A young professional walks into a bright apartment, looks around and smiles. Show the apartment keys. | "Found your perfect home?" | FOUND YOUR PERFECT HOME? |
| 4–8 sec | The landlord shares rental terms. A large security deposit amount appears. The renter pauses. | "But the upfront security deposit is holding you back?" | THE UPFRONT DEPOSIT. |
| 8–13 sec | The renter checks savings and moving expenses on a phone. | "A new home shouldn't mean putting all your savings under pressure." | MOVING COMES WITH ENOUGH EXPENSES. |
| 13–20 sec | Transition into a clean ONERENT interface. Show a conceptual deposit-financing overview. | "Introducing ONERENT. We're building a smarter way to explore rental security deposit financing options through regulated lending partners." | INTRODUCING ONERENT |
| 20–27 sec | Show: Explore options → Apply → Lender assessment → Review terms if approved. | "Explore your options, understand the applicable terms and plan your move with greater clarity." | EXPLORE. UNDERSTAND. PLAN. |
| 27–35 sec | The renter enters the new home. End on ONERENT logo and CTA. | "Your next move starts here. Join the ONERENT early-access waitlist." | JOIN THE EARLY-ACCESS WAITLIST |

**Editor direction**
- Show the apartment in the first second; no long logo intro.
- If a deposit amount is shown, label it as an illustrative example.
- Do not show automatic approval or guaranteed disbursement.
- Use a restrained blue-purple gradient for the product reveal.
- Keep the end card visible for at least three seconds.
- Music should move from mild tension to a confident, optimistic finish.

**End card**

ONERENT  
The Financial Layer of Renting.  
**Join the early-access waitlist.**

Small disclaimer: Financing availability and eligibility will depend on the regulated lending partner and applicable terms. Waitlist registration is not a loan application or approval.

# VIDEO 02 — THE DEPOSIT PROBLEM

**Duration:** 15–20 seconds  
**Format:** Quick, relatable character animation or live action.  
**Angle:** Problem-first.

**Core message:** The apartment is ready, but the upfront deposit can make moving difficult.

| Time | Visual direction | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | Renter stands inside an attractive apartment, smiling. | "Found the apartment you love?" | FOUND YOUR DREAM HOME? |

- "The hardest part of moving in can happen before you get the keys."
- "Found a home? Don't forget the upfront deposit."

# VIDEO 05 — FOUNDER / BRAND INTRODUCTION

**Duration:** 20–30 seconds  
**Format:** Real founder speaking to camera, with supporting apartment and product visuals.  
**Angle:** Human credibility.

## Production setup
- Record vertically in 9:16.
- Use a clean, naturally lit office or apartment background.
- Frame the founder from the chest upwards.
- Speak conversationally; do not use a corporate advertising voice.
- Add captions.
- Overlay relevant visuals while the founder speaks.
- Use ONERENT branding subtly.

## Founder script

"One thing about renting is that finding the right home is only the beginning.

For many people, arranging a large security deposit upfront can be a real financial challenge.

That's why we're building ONERENT — to make rental-related financial experiences easier to manage, starting with rental security deposit financing.

We're currently speaking with renters and inviting people planning their next move to join our early-access waitlist.

If this is something you've experienced, we'd love to hear from you."

## Supporting visuals

| Founder line | Supporting visual |
|---|---|
| "Finding the right home is only the beginning." | Apartment search and keys |
| "Arranging a large security deposit..." | Deposit amount and moving budget |
| "That's why we're building ONERENT..." | ONERENT logo and conceptual product interface |
| "Speaking with renters..." | Landing page waitlist form |

**End card**

ONERENT  
We're building a better rental-finance experience.  
**Join the early-access waitlist.**

**Founder delivery notes**
- Be honest that ONERENT is being built.
- Do not claim traction, partnerships or customer results that do not exist.
- Do not promise financing availability or approvals.
- Keep the delivery personal and specific.

# VIDEO 06 — UGC-STYLE RENTER SCENARIO

**Duration:** 15–25 seconds  
**Format:** Vertical 9:16, selfie-style creator video or clearly fictional actor scenario.  
**Angle:** Relatable, conversational and social-first.

**Core message:** The rental deposit is a major upfront expense that people often don't fully consider when planning a move.

## Creator script

"One thing I didn't fully think about when planning a move was the security deposit.

You budget for rent, transport and moving expenses. Then the deposit comes up, and suddenly there's a much bigger amount to arrange upfront.

That's the problem ONERENT is working on.

They're exploring a smarter way for renters to access rental security deposit financing options through lending partners.

If you're planning to rent, check out their early-access waitlist."

## Shot list

| Time | Shot | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 sec | Selfie close-up | Start with the hook immediately. No logo intro. |
| 3–8 sec | Creator gestures or shows moving boxes | Explain the deposit surprise. |
| 8–13 sec | Cut to apartment or rental paperwork | Make the situation feel real. |
| 13–19 sec | ONERENT logo and simple product visual | Explain what ONERENT is exploring. |
| 19–25 sec | Creator returns to camera or end card | Invite viewers to join the waitlist. |

**Creator guidance**
- Use natural language and a genuine conversational tone.
- Avoid scripted overacting.
- Do not present an actor's story as a real customer testimonial.
- If the creator is paid or incentivised, include appropriate advertising disclosure.
- Do not claim that the creator used ONERENT or received financing.

**Alternate hooks**
- "The rental deposit is the expense I wish I'd planned for earlier."
- "Before you move into a new apartment, remember this cost."
- "Everyone talks about monthly rent. What about the deposit?"

# MEASUREMENT CHECKLIST

Track each creative separately:

- Impressions and reach.
- Three-second video views.
- Video hold rate and completion rate.
- Outbound click-through rate.
- Landing page views.
- Waitlist form starts.
- Completed waitlist registrations.
- Cost per completed registration.
- Percentage of signups with a realistic moving timeline.
- Deposit range distribution.
- Interview acceptance and feedback.

Do not choose the winning video based on views alone. The strongest creative is the one that attracts relevant renters who understand the proposition and complete the waitlist form.
