Global rules: Space Grotesk 700 headlines (tight tracking, −2%), Manrope 500 subtitles, DM Sans for UI labels. Palette: ink #131B17, brand green #0A7B3B, accent #00E676, surface #E9F7EE, dark #08111F. One easing family (cubic-bezier 0.22, 1, 0.36, 1 for moves; 0.4 s text reveals). Screenshots sit in rounded cards (radius 24, soft shadow) and are never shown with browser chrome. Small VibeLevel mark top-left on product scenes (4–7).

Subtitles: Manrope 500, 40 px, white on a 70% #08111F pill, bottom-centre, max 2 lines / ~42 chars, timed to Whisper words. **Off** wherever the same words are the main kinetic headline (marked "no subs").

Music: "Deep Urban" (Mixkit, ~123 BPM), its quieter intro aligned to Scene 1, ducked to −18 dB under speech and up to −10 dB in gaps/transitions. SFX at −20 to −24 dB.
