# Reference style notes: TestGorilla frames (`Refernces/`)

Inspiration for **typography, characters and elements only**. Colours, logo and copy stay VibeLevel's own (green #0A7B3B / #00E676, ink #131B17, dark #08111F; Space Grotesk / Manrope).

## What they do

**Typography**
- One heavy, geometric sans in ALL CAPS, set huge (2–4 words fill ~60–70% of the frame width) with very tight leading. "ASK US WHY" stacks onto two lines.
- Text sits **over** the character, partly overlapping the figure, so it reads as part of the image rather than a caption.
- Repetition as rhythm: "WHY WHY WHY" fills the frame and scrolls vertically with motion blur.
- Big-number moments ("900 MILLION") use the same font and colour, centred over a busy collage.
- Product-UI moments switch to a calm, readable sentence-case font inside rounded cards.

**Characters**
- Single professional, chest-up, **looking straight at camera**, framed on the right half; the left half stays open for text or a card.
- Desaturated **black-and-white** city street (crosswalk, bokeh, cabs). Only the type and the UI cards carry colour, which makes the brand colour pop.
- Natural, contemporary US styling (suit, open collar, headphones, glasses). They read as real people, not stocky "business handshake" shots.
- Shallow depth of field: the background is soft, so text stays legible.

**Elements and layout**
- UI is shown as clean, **oversized rounded cards** (large radius, soft shadow) floating on a solid brand-colour or dark backdrop, never as a full screenshot with browser chrome.
- Cards are cropped tight to one idea (a prompt box, a requirements panel, a candidate card) and scaled up so their text reads at a glance.
- Evidence cards over the character ("Why he's a match" + score bars) combine person and proof in one frame.
- Transitions: perspective tilt and motion-blurred grid of cards ("wall of candidates"), whip-scroll of repeated words, hard cuts on beats.
- Solid full-bleed brand-colour backgrounds for statement frames ("ASK US WHY" white on magenta).
- A small logo is bugged top-left throughout.

## How we apply it (with VibeLevel brand)

| Their device | Our version |
|---|---|
| Huge caps headline over a B&W character | **Scene 1:** recruiter on the right in desaturated B&W; kinetic Space Grotesk 700 on the left in white with **#00E676** on the key words ("write the code", "hiring for?"). Allow slight overlap into the character's shoulder, never the face |
| Repeated-word scroll ("WHY WHY WHY") | **Scene 1 → 2 bridge:** repeat "hiring for?" or "AI can" scrolling with motion blur on the dark #08111F background before Scene 2 |
| Big-number moment | Use only real numbers from the product (e.g. report "4.9 / 10", "4 of 4 answered"). No invented stats |
| Oversized rounded UI cards on solid colour | **Scenes 4–7:** isolate report and app crops as rounded cards (radius ~24 px, soft shadow) on brand surface #E9F7EE or dark #08111F, scaled so text reads; avoid full-page screenshots |
| Evidence card next to a person | **Scene 8:** headline over a B&W background with a small genuine report card (e.g. Human Contribution ladder) as proof, no fake scores |
| Solid brand-colour statement frame | **Scenes 3 and 9:** solid brand green or #08111F with the logo, no lines or patterns (matches the brief) |
| Grid-of-cards tilt transition | **Scene 7 pull-back:** tilt the report tabs (Core Skills, Working with AI, Code Review, Video Q&A, Interview Guide) as a perspective card wall before Scene 8 |
| Persistent logo bug | Small VibeLevel mark top-left on product scenes; hide it on the full-logo frames |

## Avoid
- Magenta/pink, the gorilla mascot, their copy ("Ask us why"), and their candidate-card layout one-to-one.
- Stats we can't source from VibeLevel's own pages or the report.
