Arjun can afford his monthly rent. But paying a large deposit all at once means draining his savings, delaying his move, or borrowing from someone else.
What if renting a home came with a smarter way to manage the money behind it? Introducing Velora. A financial platform built around the renting experience.
Through Velora, eligible tenants can apply for security deposit financing through regulated lending partners. Instead of paying the entire amount upfront, they can explore a structured repayment plan that fits their financial situation.
And it doesn't stop there. Manage recurring rent payments, keep digital payment records, and access landlord protection options through insurance partners.
Now, Arjun can focus on settling into his new home, while managing his rental finances with greater clarity and confidence.
