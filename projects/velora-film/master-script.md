# VELORA — THE COMPLETE PRODUCT FILM
## One continuous cinematic film explaining the Velora ecosystem

**Brand:** Velora  
**Positioning:** The Financial Layer of Renting  
**Tagline:** Move Forward With Confidence  
**Format:** Premium 2D character animation + 2.5D motion graphics + product UI demonstrations  
**Estimated runtime:** 9–11 minutes  
**Story continuity:** Direct continuation of the original Velora brand film  
**Primary character:** Arjun, 27-year-old working professional  
**Audience:** Tenants, landlords and property managers

---

# 1. CREATIVE DIRECTION

## The central idea

The original film introduced the challenges of renting a home and presented Velora as a solution.

This film takes the audience further.

Instead of introducing four products independently, we follow Arjun through his rental journey. As his needs and responsibilities evolve, we introduce the four Velora products naturally:

1. **Velora Flex** — Rent financing.
2. **Velora Pay** — Rent payments.
3. **Velora Hub** — Rental management.
4. **Velora Protect** — Landlord rent protection options.

The audience should understand not only what each product does, but also how it works in practice.

## Visual identity

- Midnight Navy: `#111C2B`
- Warm Ivory: `#F5F0E6`
- Muted Gold: `#D9B878`
- Slate Blue: `#91A4B7`
- Typography: Inter or Manrope.
- Premium, clean financial-institution aesthetic.
- Smooth camera movements, refined UI animations and restrained transitions.
- Consistent character design and environment from the original film.

# 3. TRANSITION AND CONTINUITY PLAN

The film must feel like one uninterrupted story, not a collection of product advertisements.

| Transition | Visual connection |
|---|---|
| Original film → Flex | Original logo transitions into Arjun's apartment and his financial calendar |
| Flex → Pay | Flex repayment calendar becomes the next month's rent calendar |
| Pay → Hub | Payment history expands into the connected rental dashboard |
| Hub → Protect | Landlord dashboard introduces rental income risk |
| Protect → Ecosystem | Insurance shield transforms into the four-product ecosystem |
| Ecosystem → Final brand | Connected product lines form the Velora logo |

# 4. CHARACTER AND ENVIRONMENT CONTINUITY

**Arjun**
- Retain the exact character design from the original film.
- Same age, hairstyle, clothing style and visual proportions.
- Same apartment and furniture.
- His life should progress naturally rather than restarting in every section.

**Mr. Sharma**
- Retain the landlord character from the original story if one already exists.
- Otherwise, introduce him as a consistent supporting character.
- Maintain the same appearance throughout the film.

**Property manager**
- Introduce one supporting character.
- Keep the design simple and professional.

**Environment**
- Maintain the original apartment's colours and lighting.
- Reuse the original city and building environment.
- Use the same Velora visual identity.

# 5. PRODUCTION AND ANIMATION REQUIREMENTS

## Animation style

- Premium 2D character animation.
- 2.5D camera movement.
- Isometric financial illustrations.
- Smooth interface transitions.
- Subtle depth and parallax.
- Refined typography animation.
- Clean, realistic financial UI demonstrations.

## Camera language

Use:
- Slow push-ins for important product moments.
- Pull-backs to reveal the larger rental ecosystem.
- Match cuts between calendars, dashboards and payment timelines.
- Split-screen layouts when comparing different user experiences.
- Smooth tracking movements between characters and product interfaces.

Avoid:
- Excessive zooms.
- Random transitions.
- Overly playful character animation.
- Fast-moving text that is difficult to read.
- Repetitive product logo reveals.

## Sound design

**Opening:** Continue the original film's sonic identity.

**Velora Flex:** Subtle financial interface sounds, calendar movement and restrained ambient music.

**Velora Pay:** Light confirmation sounds, gentle notification cues and smooth payment animations.

**Velora Hub:** More connected, flowing sound design as different dashboards come together.

**Velora Protect:** Calm, confident and reassuring musical progression.

**Finale:** Build towards the same sonic logo or musical signature used in the original film.

# 7. IMPORTANT CLAIMS AND COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS

These points must be followed in the final production.

**Velora Flex**
- Financing is provided through the relevant regulated lending partner.
- Approval is subject to lender assessment and eligibility.
- Do not promise guaranteed approval, fixed interest rates or instant disbursement unless verified and contractually supported.
- Clearly disclose applicable costs and repayment obligations in the actual product journey.

**Velora Pay**
- Automatic recurring payments require valid customer authorization and supported payment arrangements.
- Manual reminders do not themselves initiate payments.
- Show successful payment only after confirmation from the relevant payment provider.
- Do not imply that every bank or payment method is supported unless confirmed.

**Velora Hub**
- Demonstrate only features that exist or are approved for the product roadmap.
- Respect role-based access and personal information privacy.
- Do not imply that all parties can access all records.

**Velora Protect**
- Insurance is provided and underwritten by the authorised insurer.
- Coverage depends on policy issuance, eligibility, exclusions and conditions.
- Do not promise guaranteed rent recovery or automatic claim approval.
- Show the insurer's claims assessment process accurately.

# 8. FINAL CREATIVE PRINCIPLE

This is not four product advertisements joined together.

It is one complete explanation of how Velora fits into the rental journey.

The audience should finish the film knowing:

- Why Velora exists.
- What Velora Flex does.
- How Velora Pay works.
- How Velora Hub connects the rental experience.
- What Velora Protect offers landlords.
- How the four products work together as one ecosystem.

**The film should be as long as it needs to be to explain these things clearly. Never sacrifice understanding for runtime.**
